## Mission briefing

Every resource in Los Santos (files, locks, sockets, GPU buffers) needs a bodyguard who never forgets to lock up. In C++ the bodyguard is an object, and its shift ends exactly at the closing brace.

**Mission:** stop writing `close()` by hand.

## Reverse order, every exit

Locals are destroyed in reverse order of construction when they leave scope, whether that's via `return`, the end of the block, or an exception unwinding the stack. So `std::lock_guard`, `std::fstream` and `std::unique_ptr` never leak, even on the early-return path you forgot about.
