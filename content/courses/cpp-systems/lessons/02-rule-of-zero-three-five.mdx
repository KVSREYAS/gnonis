## Mission briefing

A raw pointer member is a gun with no safety. Copy the class and two objects own the same memory. Destroy both and you get a double free.

**Mission:** decide who owns the gun, then write the paperwork.

## Zero is the goal

Make members `std::vector`, `std::string`, `std::unique_ptr`. The compiler generates correct copy, move and destroy, and you write nothing. Only a dedicated resource-owning class should hand-write the five.
