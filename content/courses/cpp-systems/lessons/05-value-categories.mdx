## Mission briefing

At the Move Motel, guests either have a name on the register (lvalues) or are just passing through (rvalues). The receptionist decides who can be robbed of their luggage.

**Mission:** learn which guests are fair game.

## Overloads pick the category

`f(T&)` binds lvalues, `f(T&&)` binds rvalues. `std::move` turns an lvalue into an xvalue so the `&&` overload wins.

## After the move

A moved-from standard library object is in a valid but unspecified state. You can assign to it or destroy it, but don't read its value.

Sources: [cppreference: value categories](https://en.cppreference.com/w/cpp/language/value_category), Scott Meyers, *Effective Modern C++*, Items 23–25
