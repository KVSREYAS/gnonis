## Mission briefing

The motel is expanding. Every guest has to change rooms. Move their luggage (cheap) or photocopy every item (expensive)? The manager only trusts movers who swear they'll never drop anything: `noexcept`.

**Mission:** count the photocopies.

## Count the copies

Below, `Loud` counts copies. Its move constructor is NOT noexcept.
