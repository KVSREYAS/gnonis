## Mission briefing

Some loot belongs to exactly one crew member. Hand it over and it's theirs, with no copies and no arguments.

**Mission:** use the cheapest safe in town.

## How big is it?

On x86-64 with libstdc++ or libc++, a `unique_ptr<T>` with the default deleter is 8 bytes, thanks to the empty-base / `[[no_unique_address]]` optimisation.
