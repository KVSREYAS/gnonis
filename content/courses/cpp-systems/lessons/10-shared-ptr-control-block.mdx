## Mission briefing

Some safehouses are shared. The last crew member out turns off the lights, and somebody has to keep the headcount on the wall.

**Mission:** find out what that headcount costs.

## Counting owners

Copying a shared_ptr increments the strong count (an atomic RMW). Moving it doesn't. Destroying it decrements, and at zero the object is destroyed.
