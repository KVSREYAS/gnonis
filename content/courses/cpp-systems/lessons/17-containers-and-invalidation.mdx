  - "std::vector is the default: contiguous, cache-friendly, amortised O(1) push_back (libstdc++ doubles capacity)."
  - "Reallocation invalidates all iterators, pointers and references into the vector. reserve() avoids it."
  - "Node-based containers (std::map, std::list, std::unordered_map nodes) keep element addresses stable but chase pointers."

## Mission briefing

STL Strip sells containers. The salesman always pushes the vector, and he's usually right.

**Mission:** learn when his warranty is void.
