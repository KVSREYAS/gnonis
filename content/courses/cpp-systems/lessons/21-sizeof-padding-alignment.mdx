## Why alignment

Misaligned loads can be slower, split across cache lines, or fault on some ISAs. SIMD loads like `_mm256_load_ps` require 32-byte alignment.

Sources: [cppreference: object alignment](https://en.cppreference.com/w/cpp/language/object#Alignment), [The Lost Art of Structure Packing (Eric S. Raymond)](http://www.catb.org/esr/structure-packing/)
