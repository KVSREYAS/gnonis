## Mission briefing

Every trip to DRAM is a drive across the whole map. The cache is your pockets: small, but right there.

**Mission:** make every trip count by carrying back 64 bytes you'll actually use.

## Traversal order

For `float a[N][N]`, `a[i][j]` and `a[i][j+1]` are neighbours. `a[i+1][j]` is N×4 bytes away.

Sources: Ulrich Drepper, [What Every Programmer Should Know About Memory](https://people.freebsd.org/~lstewart/articles/cpumemory.pdf), Scott Meyers, [CPU Caches and Why You Care](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WDIkqP4JbkE)
