## Mission briefing

The vault only opens if you park exactly on the line. Being off by 4 bytes means no heist.

**Mission:** park on 64-byte boundaries every time.

## The bit trick

For a power-of-two `a`, `a - 1` is a mask of the low bits. Adding `a - 1` and clearing those bits rounds up.
