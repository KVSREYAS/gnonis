## Mission briefing

You want to read the serial number stamped inside a float. Breaking in with a crowbar (reinterpret_cast) gets you arrested. The locksmith (bit_cast) does it legally, just as fast.

**Mission:** go legit.

## Float bits

An IEEE-754 float is 1 sign bit, 8 exponent bits (bias 127) and 23 mantissa bits.

![IEEE-754 float bit layout: 1 sign, 8 exponent, 23 mantissa; bf16 keeps the top 16 bits](/diagrams/cpp-systems/08-ub/02-aliasing-and-bit-cast.png)
