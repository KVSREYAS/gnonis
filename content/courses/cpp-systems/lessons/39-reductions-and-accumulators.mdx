keyIdeas:
  - "FP add latency is ~4 cycles on modern x86, and throughput is ~2 per cycle. One chain uses a fraction of that."
  - "K independent accumulators hide latency: you need about latency × throughput of them (4 × 2 = 8)."
  - "This changes the rounding order. That's fine for ML, but document it."
