  - "Dead-code elimination: if the result isn't used, the loop may vanish. Use benchmark::DoNotOptimize, or consume the result."
  - "Warm up, repeat, and report the median or minimum with spread. Pin the CPU frequency or at least note it."
  - "Microbenchmarks lie about caches: a 4 KB working set lives in L1, while production data might not."
