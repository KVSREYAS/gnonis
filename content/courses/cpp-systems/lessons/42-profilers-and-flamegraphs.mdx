keyIdeas:
  - "`perf stat` gives counters (cycles, instructions, IPC, cache-misses, branch-misses). `perf record -g` plus `perf report` samples call stacks."
  - "Flame graphs: width is time on CPU, and the y-axis is stack depth. Look for wide plateaus."
  - "IPC well below 1 usually means memory stalls. Near 3–4 means you're compute-bound."
