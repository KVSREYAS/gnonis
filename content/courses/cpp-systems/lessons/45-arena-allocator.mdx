keyIdeas:
  - "An arena owns one big buffer. Allocation is align_up(offset) then bump, which is O(1) with no per-object free."
  - "Reset frees everything at once, which suits per-request or per-step lifetimes."
  - "std::pmr::monotonic_buffer_resource is the standard arena and plugs into std::pmr containers."
