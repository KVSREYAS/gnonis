## Revenue, cost and profit

Three words you need for the game:

- **Revenue**: the money that comes in. Price times the number sold.
- **Cost**: the money that goes out. Here, ₹5 for every cup you make, sold or not.
- **Profit**: revenue minus cost.

Customers drop away as the price rises: in this town nobody pays ₹40 or more. Hotter days bring more people. You see the forecast before you open.

## What the numbers showed

Two mistakes cost money in this game:

1. **Wasted cups**: you made more than people wanted, and each unsold cup still cost ₹5.
2. **Turned-away customers**: you made too few, and lost sales that would have paid well above ₹5.

Raising the price earns more per cup but sells fewer cups. The best price is where one more rupee of price no longer earns more than the sales it loses. That is marginal thinking in action.
