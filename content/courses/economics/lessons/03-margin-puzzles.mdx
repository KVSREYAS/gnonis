## Marginal tables

A bakery adds workers. The table shows total loaves per day.

| Workers | Loaves | Extra loaves |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 50 | 50 |
| 2 | 90 | 40 |
| 3 | 120 | 30 |
| 4 | 140 | 20 |
| 5 | 150 | 10 |

The **marginal product** is the extra output from one more worker. It falls as more workers share the same oven. This is called **diminishing marginal returns**.

## Free is not always free

A shop offers "buy 2, get the 3rd free". If you only wanted one shirt, the marginal cost of the second shirt is its full price, and the marginal benefit is whatever a second shirt is worth to you. The free third shirt only matters if you value it.

Offers like this work because people compare the deal to the total price instead of asking what each extra item is worth to them.
