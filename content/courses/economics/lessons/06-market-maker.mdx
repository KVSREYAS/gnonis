## How to play

You run the price board for a market of chai stalls. Move the slider and watch two numbers:

- **Buyers want**: quantity demanded at your price.
- **Sellers offer**: quantity supplied at your price.

If buyers want more, there is a shortage, so raise the price. If sellers offer more, there is a surplus, so cut it. Press **Set price** when you think it clears. Then news hits the market and you do it again.

## Reading the shifts

![Demand shifting right raises both the price and the quantity sold](/diagrams/economics/demand-shift.png)

What you just saw:

| News | Curve | Price | Quantity |
|---|---|---|---|
| More buyers | Demand right | Up | Up |
| Higher input cost | Supply left | Up | Down |
| More sellers | Supply right | Down | Up |

When price and quantity move the same way, look for a demand shift. When they move opposite ways, look for a supply shift.
