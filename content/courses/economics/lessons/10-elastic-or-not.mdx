## How to play

Each card shows a price change and what happened to the quantity sold. Call it **elastic** (buyers reacted more than the price moved) or **inelastic** (less). The card then shows the midpoint elasticity and why.

You can do the maths, but try calling it from the type of good first. That is the skill that sticks.

## What to remember

The same good can be inelastic in the short run and elastic in the long run. Petrol is the classic case: next week you still drive to work, but over ten years you can buy an EV or move closer.

A whole category (any soft drink) is less elastic than one brand inside it (one cola), because brands substitute for each other.
