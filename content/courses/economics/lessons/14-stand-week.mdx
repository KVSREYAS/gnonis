## The full stand

Same rules as module 1, now for a week:

- Each cup costs ₹5 to make, sold or not.
- Nobody pays ₹40 or more.
- Customers by weather: rainy 30, cloudy 60, sunny 100, heatwave 150, before price puts some off.

The forecast is exact. At a price p, the share who buy is (1 − p/40). Use that to choose how many cups to make.

## Why the best price did not change

With a fixed cost per cup and demand that scales up and down with the weather, the profit-maximising price is the same every day. Weather moves the demand curve outward, so you sell more cups at the same price.

That is MR = MC in a small form: the price where one more rupee of price stops paying off depends on the shape of demand and the cost per cup, not on the crowd size.
