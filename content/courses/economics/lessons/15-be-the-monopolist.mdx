## Your market

You hold the only licence to run ferries across a river. Demand is P = 100 − Q (Q is trips a day), and each trip costs ₹20 in fuel and wages.

The game shows units sold, your profit, **buyers' surplus** (how much more buyers would have paid than they did, added up) and **deadweight loss**. Find the price with the highest profit.

## The cost of market power

At ₹60 you earn ₹1,600 a day. Buyers' surplus is ₹800, and ₹800 of possible gains is lost (the deadweight loss triangle). Under competition at ₹20, buyers' surplus would be ₹3,200 and nothing would be lost.

This is why governments use **competition law**: to stop mergers that create monopolies, and to regulate natural monopolies like power grids.
