## The puzzle

Two islands, 10 working hours a day each.

- **Asha** makes 4 kg rice or 2 m cloth per hour.
- **Ben** makes 1 kg rice or 3 m cloth per hour.

Asha needs 20 kg rice and 12 m cloth. Ben needs 8 kg rice and 14 m cloth. Try it alone first: neither can manage. Then specialise and trade. Set how much rice Asha sends Ben and the cloth price per kg.

## Why it worked

Alone, each island can only reach a mix on its own frontier. Asha would need 11 hours to cover her needs, Ben nearly 13. By specialising, the total of both goods goes up, because each hour goes to whoever gives up the least to make that good.

The trade price splits the gain. A price near Asha's cost (0.5) gives most of the gain to Ben, a price near Ben's (3) gives most to Asha. Any price in between leaves both better off than going alone.
