## Setup

An Indian software firm and a Vietnamese firm each have 8 engineer-days.

- **India** writes 6 apps or tests 6 apps per day.
- **Vietnam** writes 1 app or tests 3 apps per day.

India is better at both. India needs 36 apps written and 16 tested. Vietnam needs 4 written and 13 tested. Neither can do it alone. Find the split and the trade.

## Takeaway

Comparative advantage, not absolute advantage, decides who should do what. A top surgeon who types fast still hires a typist, because each hour typing costs her an hour of surgery.

The same logic explains why rich and poor countries both gain from trading with each other. The gains are not always shared evenly inside each country: workers in industries that shrink can lose, which is why trade policy is debated.
