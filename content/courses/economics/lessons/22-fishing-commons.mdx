## The lake

The lake holds up to 1,000 fish. Each season the fish left over regrow: fastest when the lake is half full (125 new fish), slowly when nearly empty or nearly full.

![Regrowth peaks at 500 fish](/diagrams/economics/commons-growth.png)

Four boats share it. The sustainable total is about 125 fish a season, roughly 31 per boat. The other three boats start near a fair share, but they copy any overfishing they see from you.

## What happened

If you took a lot early, the others followed and the stock crashed, so everyone's long-run catch fell. This is the tragedy of the commons in action.

Elinor Ostrom won the 2009 Nobel Prize for showing that real communities often avoid this: they set catch limits, watch each other, and punish cheats. Governments also use quotas and tradable fishing rights. The common thread is that someone limits access.
