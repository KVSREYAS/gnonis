## The setup

In this market a "lemon" is a bad used car. Each car has a quality from ₹0 to ₹100 thousand: what it is worth to its owner. It is worth 1.5 times that to you, so every trade could make both sides richer.

But only the owner knows the quality. An owner sells only if your offer is at least what the car is worth to them. So at an offer of ₹60k, only cars worth ₹0 to ₹60k get sold, and their average is ₹30k.

## Why the blind market collapses

At any blind offer P, you get an average car worth 0.75 P to you but pay P. You lose a quarter of your offer on average, so the only safe offer is zero, and no cars trade. Gains that both sides wanted disappear because of missing information.

Inspections, certified pre-owned schemes, warranties and seller ratings exist to fix exactly this. Akerlof shared the 2001 Nobel Prize for this idea.
