## The race

Goal: ₹50 lakh in **today's money** (real value). Inflation is 6%. You choose a monthly deposit, a yearly return, and the number of years.

The chart shows three lines: what the account shows on paper, what that is worth in today's money, and the total you paid in.

## What the race shows

Doubling the deposit doubles the final amount. Adding years does more, because each extra year compounds everything before it. The gap between the gold and green lines is inflation: after 30 years at 6%, a rupee buys only about a sixth of what it does today.

Real investments do not pay a fixed return each year. Higher average returns usually come with more risk.
