## Who wins and who loses

Unexpected inflation moves wealth around:

- **Savers and lenders lose** when the interest rate does not keep up.
- **Borrowers with fixed rates gain**, because they repay with rupees that buy less.
- **People on fixed incomes lose**, such as pensions not linked to prices.

When lenders expect inflation, they ask for higher nominal rates to protect the real rate. This link is called the **Fisher effect**.

## Takeaway

A deposit is safe in nominal terms: the number never falls. In real terms it can lose value every year that inflation is above the interest rate, and tax on the interest makes it worse. That is why long-term savers often hold assets whose returns have tended to beat inflation, while keeping deposits for short-term needs.
