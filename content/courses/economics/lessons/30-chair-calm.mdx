## Your job

In this simulator the target is 2% inflation and 5% unemployment. Each quarter you announce a policy rate from 0% to 15%.

How the model works:

- The **real rate** is your rate minus expected inflation. A real rate of 1% is **neutral**: it neither speeds up nor slows the economy.
- Above neutral, demand cools. Below it, demand heats up.
- Hot demand raises inflation and lowers unemployment.
- Expected inflation moves halfway toward actual inflation each quarter.

## What you learned

Rates work with a lag: what you set this quarter mostly affects the next few. Waiting for inflation to show up means you are already late, and then you have to move harder.

Real central banks face the same problem, with worse data and uncertain lags. That is why they talk so much about forecasts and expectations.
