## The show

A game show has 3 doors. A car is behind one, goats behind the other two. You pick a door. The host, who knows where the car is, then opens one of the other doors to show a goat, and offers you a switch to the last closed door.

Should you switch? Most people say it does not matter, 50/50. Play a few rounds and keep score. When you have played enough, the simulator unlocks and plays 10,000 games of each choice.

## Why switching wins

Your first pick is the car 1/3 of the time. In those games, switching loses. The other 2/3 of the time your first pick is a goat, so the host is forced to open the only other goat, and the door he leaves closed **must** be the car. Switching wins every one of those games.

So switching wins 2/3. The host is not choosing at random: his choice is restricted by where the car is, and that restriction is information.
