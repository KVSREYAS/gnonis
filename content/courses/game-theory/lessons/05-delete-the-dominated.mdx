## How to play

Rows are your strategies (your payoffs in gold). Columns are the other player's (their payoffs in green). Tap a row to delete it only if another remaining row gives you **more in every remaining column**. Tap a column only if another remaining column gives **them** more in every remaining row.

Wrong taps cost a mistake. Two mistakes and you replay.

## What you just did

No strategy here was dominant at the start. But Right was always worse than Centre for the column player, so a rational column player never plays it. Once you know that, Bottom stops being attractive for you, and once Bottom is gone, Left stops being attractive for them.

Each step used one more layer of reasoning about the other player's reasoning. The next game tests how many layers real people use.
