## How to play

For each table, tap every cell that is a Nash equilibrium, then press Check. If you think there is none, press Check with nothing selected.

Reminder: gold is the row player's payoff, green is the column player's. A cell is an equilibrium if gold is the highest in its **column** and green is the highest in its **row**.

## What the tables showed

- One equilibrium (prisoner's dilemma) can be bad for both.
- Two equilibria (stag hunt, cinema) leave a coordination problem: which one?
- Zero pure equilibria (matching pennies) means players should be unpredictable.
- In the price war, High/High pays both the most (6, 6) but is not stable: each firm gains 2 by dropping to Medium. Medium/Medium (4, 4) and Low/Low (2, 2) are both equilibria, and once firms are stuck at Low, neither gains by raising its price alone.
