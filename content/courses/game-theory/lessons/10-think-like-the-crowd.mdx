## How to play

Each question has no right answer except the popular one. You score a point if you choose the option that most other players chose. The percentages shown after each answer are typical results from class polls of this kind of question. Schelling's own heads-or-tails result was that most people choose heads.

Do not pick what you like. Pick what you think everyone else will think everyone else will pick.

## Why focal points matter

Many real coordination problems are solved this way: which side of the road to drive on, which messaging app a group uses, which technical standard wins. Once most people pick one equilibrium, it stays put even if another would be better, because no single person gains by switching alone.

That is also why first movers and defaults are powerful.
