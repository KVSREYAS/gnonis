## How to play

You take 20 penalties. The scoring chances are the real-data table from the last lesson. The keeper watches your last 5 kicks and leans toward your more common side.

If you always go to your best side, the keeper learns it and your scoring rate falls. Score 14 or more to win.

## What you were up against

Humans are bad at being random: we switch too often and avoid streaks. A keeper (or a computer) that tracks patterns punishes that.

The equilibrium idea is not that you flip a coin for fun. It is that any predictability is a weakness the other side can use, so the only safe plan is one they cannot read.
