## How to play

Each box is a decision. Tap the move the player in that box would really choose. The first number of each payoff is gold's, the second is green's. Answer **every** box, including ones that end up off the main path.

Warm up with the entry game from the last lesson, then a harder product launch.

## Now three levels

In the next tree you launch a product (big, small, or not at all). Your rival then decides whether to copy you. After a big launch that gets copied, you choose whether to start a price war. Work from the bottom: what do you do after being copied? Then what does the rival do? Then what launch do you choose?

## Notice

The rival copies a big launch even though ignoring would give **you** 6. And your threat of a price war after being copied is empty: when the moment comes, you would keep prices. Backward induction strips away threats and promises that nobody would follow through on.
