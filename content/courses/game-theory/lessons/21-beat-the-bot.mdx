## How to play

You play 10 rounds against a bot. Both cooperate: 3 each. Both defect: 1 each. You defect on a cooperator: 5 for you, 0 for it.

The first bot plays a strategy you can see. Get 27 points. The second bot hides its strategy. Score 30 and then guess what it was. You will need to test it.

## Now the mystery bot

The next bot plays one of the strategies from the concepts lesson. To tell them apart, try a defection and watch the reply. Try two in a row. Then settle into whatever pays best.

## What you learned

Forgiving strategies can be exploited by a player who defects just often enough not to trigger them. Strict ones like grim trigger cannot be exploited, but never recover from a single slip. That trade-off is the theme of the next lesson.
