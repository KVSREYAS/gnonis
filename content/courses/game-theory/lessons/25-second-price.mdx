## How to play

Each round you see your value for the item. Three bots also have private values, and in this format they bid them. Submit a sealed bid. If you win, you pay the second-highest bid and your profit is your value minus that price. End with at least ₹15 profit over 10 rounds.

Try overbidding once just to see it go wrong.

## What happened

Every time you bid your value you either won at a price below your value, or lost to someone who valued it more. Overbidding only mattered when it made you win at a price above your value. That is the dominant-strategy argument, played out.
