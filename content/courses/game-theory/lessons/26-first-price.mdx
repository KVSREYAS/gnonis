## How to play

Same values and same rivals, but now the winner pays **their own** bid. The bots shade to about 3/4 of their values, with some noise. Make at least ₹15 profit over 10 rounds.

## Compare

If you averaged your profit per round in both formats, they were probably close. That is revenue equivalence from the buyer's side: the seller gets about the same either way, so bidders do too.

Real sellers still choose formats carefully: first-price is harder to rig with fake bids, second-price is easier for bidders to get right.
