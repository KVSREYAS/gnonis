## How to play

Part 1: you propose. Five bots each have a secret minimum they will accept out of 10 coins. Offer too little and both get 0.

Part 2: you respond. Four bots make you offers. Accept and get the offer, or reject and both get 0.

End with at least 33 coins. Fair 5/5 offers to everyone get you 25 from part 1. You can do better if you read the bots.

## What this shows

As a responder, rejecting cost you real coins. As a proposer, the best offer depended on what each bot would accept, not on theory alone. In real life you rarely know the minimum, so offers near 40-50% are the safe choice, which is what most people do.
