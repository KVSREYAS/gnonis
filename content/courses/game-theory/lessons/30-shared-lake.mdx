## The lake

This is the tragedy of the commons as a game. The lake holds up to 1,000 fish and regrows fastest when half full: 125 new fish a season. Six boats share it, so a sustainable share is about 21 fish per boat per season.

The five other boats start close to a fair share but copy any overfishing they see from you.

## How groups fix it

Elinor Ostrom studied fisheries, forests and irrigation systems that lasted for centuries. Successful ones had clear boundaries, rules made by the users, monitoring, gradual punishments for cheats, and cheap ways to settle disputes. In game terms, they turned a one-shot dilemma into a repeated game with punishment, which is exactly what makes cooperation an equilibrium.
