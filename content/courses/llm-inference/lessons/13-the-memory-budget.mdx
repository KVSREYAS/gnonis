## Mission briefing

Weights pay rent once. The KV cache is the roommate who keeps inviting friends.

**Mission:** You've got one 80 GB garage. Park the weights, then fit as many customers' caches as you can.

## Weights first, cache second

A serving GPU's memory splits into three parts:

1. **Weights**: fixed. 8B parameters in fp16 take 16 GB.
2. **Activations and workspace**: temporary buffers, usually small.
3. **KV cache**: everything that is left.

vLLM, for example, claims `gpu_memory_utilization = 0.9` of the GPU by default. After loading
weights and profiling activations, the remainder becomes the pool of KV blocks.

## Long contexts eat concurrency

At 32k tokens, one Llama 3 8B sequence needs 4.3 GB of fp16 cache. Long-context products hit the
wall fast. The levers are fewer bytes per element, fewer KV heads, shorter contexts, or more
memory.

## Read the config right

Most estimators read a Hugging Face `config.json`. The field names matter: `num_attention_heads`
counts query heads, while `num_key_value_heads` counts KV heads.
