keyIdeas:
  - Arithmetic intensity = FLOPs / bytes, and the roofline is min(peak,
    intensity x bandwidth).
  - Batch-1 decode is about 1 FLOP/byte in fp16, far below any GPU's ridge point.
  - Decode ceiling ≈ bandwidth / bytes read per step. Batching and quantization
    move you up.
