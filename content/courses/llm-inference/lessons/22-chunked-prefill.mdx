## Mission briefing

Some clown parked an 8,000-token prompt across the intersection, and 96 cars behind him are honking.

**Mission:** cut the truck into pieces small enough that traffic keeps flowing.

## The prefill stall

Continuous batching mixes new prefills with ongoing decodes. A huge prefill can take hundreds of milliseconds of compute, and every decoding user waits for that whole step. Their inter-token latency spikes, and streams visibly stutter.

## Chop it up

Sarathi-Serve (OSDI 2024) splits prefills into chunks and builds each step from a fixed token budget: all running decodes first, then a prefill chunk to fill the rest. vLLM and SGLang ship this as chunked prefill.

## How many chunks?

Chunk size trades TTFT for smooth decoding. Smaller chunks keep decode steps short, but the long prompt takes more steps to finish.

Sources: [Sarathi-Serve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.02310), [vLLM chunked prefill docs](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/latest/configuration/optimization.html)
