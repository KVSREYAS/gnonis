## Mission briefing

Paged Pier is full. A new yacht shows up and the harbour master needs a berth now.

**Mission:** decide who gets towed, and whether to tow them to the CPU lot or just sink them and rebuild later.

## Know your pool

At startup vLLM profiles memory, subtracts weights and activations, and carves the rest into fixed blocks. Every running sequence needs ceil(tokens / 16) of them, plus room to grow.

## Preempt: swap or recompute

When decoding needs a block and none are free, vLLM preempts the newest sequences. Swap mode copies their blocks to CPU RAM. Recompute mode frees them and reruns the prefill later. Prefill is compute-efficient, so recompute is often cheaper than PCIe traffic.

Source: [vLLM paper §4.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.06180)
