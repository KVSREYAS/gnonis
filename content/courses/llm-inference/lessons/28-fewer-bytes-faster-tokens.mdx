## Mission briefing

The cops at the bridge weigh every truck. Your 140 GB fp16 cargo won't fit on one 80 GB GPU. Repack it
into 4-bit crates, keep the goods intact, and drive past while they're still counting.

## Fewer bytes, faster tokens

Batch-1 decode is bandwidth divided by bytes read. Halve the bytes per weight and the ceiling roughly doubles.

## The methods

| Method | What gets quantized | Trick |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GPTQ | weights (3-4 bit) | second-order error correction |
| AWQ | weights (4 bit) | protect salient channels |
| SmoothQuant | weights + activations (int8) | migrate outliers into weights |
| FP8 | weights + activations (+KV) | hardware-native float format |

## Floats on a diet

FP8 comes in two flavours. E4M3 has more precision and E5M2 has more range.

## Street wisdom

Quantization is JPEG for weights. Done well, nobody notices. Done badly, the model starts recommending
you drive into the ocean.
