## Mission briefing

The smuggling crates hold 256 slots (int8) or 16 slots (int4). Your cargo comes in every size imaginable.

**Mission:** choose a ruler (the scale) so nothing important gets crushed.

## The scale

Symmetric quantization stretches the integer grid over [−max|w|, +max|w|]. One step is the scale. Each weight rounds to the nearest step, so the error is at most half a step.

## Groups

With one scale per tensor, a single big outlier makes every step huge. Per-channel or per-group scales (GPTQ and AWQ commonly use 128) keep steps small. You pay with extra scale bytes.

Sources: [GPTQ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.17323), [AWQ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.00978)
