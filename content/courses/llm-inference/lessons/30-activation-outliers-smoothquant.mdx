## Monster channels

Dettmers et al. (LLM.int8()) showed that above about 6.7B parameters, a handful of hidden dimensions carry huge activation values. Naive int8 activation quantization destroys accuracy. Their fix keeps those channels in fp16, which is accurate but slower.

## Smooth it over

SmoothQuant divides each activation channel by s_j and multiplies the matching weight row by s_j. With α = 0.5 the difficulty is split evenly. Both sides then quantize well to int8.

Sources: [LLM.int8()](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.07339), [SmoothQuant](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.10438)
