## Mission briefing

The warehouse is the KV cache, and it's bursting with customers' bags.

**Mission:** vacuum-pack every bag so twice as many customers fit inside.

## Why the cache

At long contexts and high concurrency the KV cache dwarfs the weights. Halving its bytes doubles how many sequences fit and halves the KV bytes read per decode step.

## Going lower

FP8 (E4M3) KV is production-ready in vLLM and TensorRT-LLM. KIVI shows 2-bit KV with per-channel keys and per-token values, keeping recent tokens in full precision.

Sources: [vLLM quantized KV cache](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/latest/features/quantization/quantized_kvcache.html), [KIVI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02750)
