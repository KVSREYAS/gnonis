## Mission briefing

Every time you need a tool you drive to the garage across town (HBM), drop off a crate the size of a house, then drive back to fetch it.

**Mission:** find out how much of the night you spend in traffic.

## The N² crates

Naive attention runs as separate kernels, and each one writes its N×N result to HBM for the next to read. With 4k tokens and 32 heads that's a GiB per matrix per layer.

## IO, not FLOPs

Dao et al. counted HBM accesses: Θ(Nd + N²) for standard attention versus Θ(N²d²/M) for FlashAttention, where M is the SRAM size. Same maths, far less traffic.

Source: [FlashAttention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14135)
