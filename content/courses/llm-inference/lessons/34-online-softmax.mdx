## Running max, running sum

Milakov and Gimelshein (2018) showed softmax can be computed in one pass. Keep m (the max so far) and l (the sum of exp(x − m)). When a bigger value arrives, multiply l by exp(m_old − m_new) and carry on.

## Why fp16 overflows

fp16's largest finite value is 65,504, and e¹¹ ≈ 59,874 is already close to it. So in fp16, exp of any score above about 11 becomes infinity, and infinity divided by infinity is NaN.

Softmax doesn't change if every score shifts by the same amount, because the common factor cancels in the division. Subtracting the row's max makes the largest term e⁰ = 1 and every other term smaller than 1, so nothing can overflow.

Online softmax keeps this safety while reading scores in tiles: it tracks the running max and rescales the running sum whenever the max grows.

## Merge two tiles

The same rule merges any two partial results, which is what lets FlashAttention process K/V tiles one at a time.

Sources: [Online softmax](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.02867), [FlashAttention §3.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14135)
