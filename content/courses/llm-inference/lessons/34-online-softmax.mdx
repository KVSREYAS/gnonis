## Mission briefing

You're counting stolen cash as the bags come in, but the exchange rate (the max) keeps changing.

**Mission:** keep a running total you can fix up without recounting old bags.

## Running max, running sum

Milakov and Gimelshein (2018) showed softmax can be computed in one pass. Keep m (the max so far) and l (the sum of exp(x − m)). When a bigger value arrives, multiply l by exp(m_old − m_new) and carry on.

## Merge two tiles

The same rule merges any two partial results, which is what lets FlashAttention process K/V tiles one at a time.

Sources: [Online softmax](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.02867), [FlashAttention §3.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14135)
