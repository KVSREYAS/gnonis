## Mission briefing

The glovebox (SRAM) is tiny but right next to you. The garage (HBM) is huge and miles away.

**Mission:** plan the job so each tool comes out of the garage once, and the big crate never goes back.

## Tiles

Split Q into row blocks and K/V into column blocks. For each Q block, stream K/V blocks through SRAM, compute the scores, update the online softmax stats and accumulate O. Only O (N × d) goes back to HBM.

## Versions

FA2 (2023) parallelises over the sequence and cuts non-matmul FLOPs, about 2x faster than FA1. FA3 (2024) uses Hopper's TMA and warp specialisation plus FP8.

Sources: [FA2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08691), [FA3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08608)
