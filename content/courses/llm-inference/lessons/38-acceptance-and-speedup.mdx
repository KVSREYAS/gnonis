## Mission briefing

The BMX scout is fast but not free: every guess costs a bit of time.

**Mission:** work out how far ahead he should scout before the guessing costs more than it saves.

## Tokens per pass

If each drafted token is accepted with probability α and you draft γ, the target emits on average (1 − α^(γ+1)) / (1 − α) tokens per pass, counting the correction token.

## Paying the scout

Drafting γ tokens costs γc target-passes of time. The speedup is the ratio of the two. Larger γ helps only while α^γ is still meaningful.

Source: [Leviathan et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.17192)
