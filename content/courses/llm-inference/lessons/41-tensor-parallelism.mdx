## Mission briefing

The job is too big for one crew. Split the safe-cracking: each guy drills a quarter of the door, then everybody shouts their progress at once (the all-reduce).

**Mission:** count how much shouting that takes.

## Column, then row

Megatron-LM shards the first MLP weight by columns (each GPU computes part of the hidden layer) and the second by rows (partial outputs), then sums them with one all-reduce. Attention splits by heads the same way.

## The shouting bill

Each all-reduce moves about twice the activation size per GPU. That's fast on NVLink and painful across nodes.

Source: [Megatron-LM](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.08053)
