missionIntro: Give each GPU a slice of the layers and pass activations from one GPU
  to the next. Idle time (bubbles) is the main cost.
keyIdeas:
  - Pipeline parallelism (PP) assigns contiguous layer ranges to stages and
    sends activations point to point.
  - With p stages and m micro-batches, the idle bubble fraction is (p − 1) / (m
    + p − 1).
  - PP communication is light, so it scales across nodes well. Combine TP inside
    nodes with PP across them.

## Stages

Each stage owns a block of layers. Stage k finishes a micro-batch and sends its activations to stage k + 1. In inference, different requests or micro-batches fill the pipeline.

## Bubbles

At the start and end some stages sit idle. With p stages and m equal micro-batches, the last micro-batch leaves the last stage after m + p − 1 time slots. Each stage is busy for m of those slots, so the idle share is (p − 1) / (m + p − 1), the formula from GPipe. More micro-batches shrink the bubble.

Sources: [GPipe](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.06965), [Megatron-LM PP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.04473)
