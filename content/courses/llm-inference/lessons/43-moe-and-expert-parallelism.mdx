## Mission briefing

Shard City's gang has 256 specialists, but each job only calls in 8. Payroll (memory) is huge, the job itself is cheap.

**Mission:** figure out who to keep on speed dial.

## Total vs active

A router picks the top-k experts per token. Mixtral uses 8 experts with top-2. DeepSeek-V3 uses 256 routed experts plus 1 shared, with 8 active. You need memory for the total and bandwidth for the active.

## Batches touch more experts

At batch 1 only k experts are read. At larger batches, different tokens pick different experts, and soon nearly all are read every step.

## Expert parallelism

EP spreads experts across GPUs. Tokens are dispatched all-to-all to their experts and combined afterwards. Load balance becomes the headache.

Sources: [Mixtral](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04088), [DeepSeek-V3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19437)
