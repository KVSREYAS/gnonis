## Mission briefing

The kitchen at Cluckin' Bell has one grill for big orders and one fryer for drip-feeding fries, and they keep fighting over it.

**Mission:** build two kitchens and a conveyor between them.

## Interference

In one engine, a long prefill delays every decode step (worse TPOT), and decodes eat into prefill time (worse TTFT). Chunked prefill softens this. Disaggregation removes it.

## Two pools and a conveyor

The prefill pool computes the KV and ships it over NVLink or RDMA to the decode pool. Each pool picks its own GPU count and parallelism.

Sources: [DistServe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.09670), [Splitwise](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18677), [Mooncake](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.00079)
