## Mission briefing

The bouncer at the club door is your scheduler. Too strict and the club is empty, too loose and the fire marshal shuts you down.

**Mission:** write the bouncer.

## Decodes first

Running users are mid-conversation, so they always get their token. Their cost comes out of the step budget first, and new prompts get whatever is left.

  solution={"import math\n\ndef schedule_step(running, waiting, budget, free_blocks, block_size=16):\n    budget -= len(running)\n    admitted = []\n    for p in waiting:\n        need = math.ceil(p / block_size)\n        if p > budget or need > free_blocks:\n            break\n        admitted.append(p)\n        budget -= p\n        free_blocks -= need\n    return admitted, budget, free_blocks\n"}
