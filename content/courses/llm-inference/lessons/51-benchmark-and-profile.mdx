## Mission briefing

The club's open. Now find out how many people it holds before the queue spills into the street.

**Mission:** load-test it like a stress-testing maniac.

## Sweep the load

Fire requests at increasing rates (Poisson arrivals) and record per-request TTFT and TPOT. Plot p50 and p99 against the rate. The knee is your capacity.

![p50 and p99 latency against request rate, flat then shooting up at the saturation knee](/diagrams/llm-inference/12-capstone/03-benchmark-and-profile.png)

## Check the roofline

At batch 1, compare tokens/s × bytes read per token to peak bandwidth. Far below 60% means overhead: Python, kernel launches, sampling.
