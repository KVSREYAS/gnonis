      import math

      def softmax(logits, temperature=1.0):
          scaled = [x / temperature for x in logits]
          m = max(scaled)
          exps = [math.exp(x - m) for x in scaled]
          z = sum(exps)
          return [e / z for e in exps]

## Softmax

Logits can be any real numbers. **Softmax** turns them into probabilities:

$$p_i = \frac{e^{z_i}}{\sum_j e^{z_j}}$$

Bigger logits get exponentially more probability, and everything sums to 1. In practice you
subtract $\max(z)$ from every logit first: the answer is identical, but $e^{1000}$ no longer
overflows.

**Greedy decoding** skips sampling and just takes the argmax. It is deterministic, but it tends
to repeat itself on long outputs.

## Temperature, top-k, top-p

Samplers reshape the distribution before drawing from it:

- **Temperature $T$** divides every logit by $T$. $T < 1$ sharpens, $T > 1$ flattens, and
  $T \to 0$ becomes greedy.
- **Top-k** keeps only the $k$ most likely tokens.
- **Top-p** (nucleus) keeps the smallest set whose probabilities add up to $p$.

After filtering, the survivors are renormalised and one is sampled at random. Try the knobs below.
