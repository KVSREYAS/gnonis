## Mission briefing

You can only think so fast and fetch so fast. Every kernel is pinned against one of those ceilings.

**Mission:** Two walls, one plot. Find out which one your kernel keeps crashing into.

## Two ceilings

The **roofline model** plots attainable performance against arithmetic intensity on log-log axes:

$$P = \min(P_{peak},\; I \times BW)$$

On the left, the sloped roof: you are **memory-bound** and speed equals bandwidth times
intensity. On the right, the flat roof: you are **compute-bound** at peak FLOP/s.

## The ridge point

Where the two roofs meet, $I^* = P_{peak} / BW$. Kernels left of it waste compute, and kernels
right of it waste bandwidth.

| GPU | Peak dense BF16 / FP16 | HBM bandwidth | Ridge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L4 | 121 TFLOP/s | 0.3 TB/s | ~400 |
| A100 80GB | 312 TFLOP/s | 2.0 TB/s | ~156 |
| H100 SXM | 989 TFLOP/s | 3.35 TB/s | ~295 |

## Move the dot

Batch-1 decode sits at about 1 FLOP/byte, far left, using well under 1% of an H100's compute.
Drag the batch size and watch the dot climb the slope.
