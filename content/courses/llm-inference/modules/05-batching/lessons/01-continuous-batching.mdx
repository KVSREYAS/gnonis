## Mission briefing

CJ, the bus to Batch Boulevard leaves when *everyone* is aboard. Problem: one passenger is writing a
1,000-token novel while three others wanted a "yes". Old-school **static batching** makes the whole bus
wait. Your job: run a bus that picks up and drops off riders at **every stop**.

## Why batch at all?

At batch 1, decode reads every weight to produce **one** token (you met this on Roofline Ridge). Put
32 sequences in the batch and the same weight read produces **32** tokens. Throughput climbs almost
linearly until you hit the compute roof or run out of KV memory.

## The slowpoke problem

In a static batch, every seat stays reserved until the longest request finishes.

## Iteration-level scheduling

Orca (OSDI 2022) proposed scheduling per **iteration**: after every forward pass, finished sequences
leave and waiting ones join. vLLM, TGI, SGLang and TensorRT-LLM ("in-flight batching") all do this.

## Street wisdom

Static batching is a restaurant that seats a table of four and won't seat anyone else until the slowest
eater finishes dessert. Continuous batching is a sushi conveyor belt.
