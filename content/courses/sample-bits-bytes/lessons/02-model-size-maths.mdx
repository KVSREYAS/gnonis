## The one-liner

$$\text{weight bytes} = \text{parameters} \times \text{bytes per parameter}$$

An 8B model is 16 GB in bf16, 8 GB in int8 and 4 GB in int4. Quantized formats add a little for
scale factors, often a few percent.
