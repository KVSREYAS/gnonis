## Bits on a budget

Every weight and activation is stored in some number format. For inference the common ones are:

| Format | Bits | Bytes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| fp32 | 32 | 4 |
| fp16 / bf16 | 16 | 2 |
| fp8 / int8 | 8 | 1 |
| int4 | 4 | 0.5 |

**bf16** keeps the 8 exponent bits of fp32 and drops mantissa precision. That makes it hard to
overflow, which is why most models are trained and served in it.

## Why it matters

Bytes are what you pay for twice: once in **capacity** (does it fit?) and once in **bandwidth**
(how fast can you read it?). Halving the format halves both.
